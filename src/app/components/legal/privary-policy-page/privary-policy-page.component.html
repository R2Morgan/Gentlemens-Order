Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2025

At Gentlemen’s Order ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at www.gentlemens-order.com, your privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal data that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter or communications.
  • Name — if provided during subscription.
  • Consent information — when you agree to receive communications from us.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as health, financial, or identification information).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To send newsletters, exclusive content, and updates about Gentlemen’s Order.
  • To deliver style tips, product updates, and promotional offers you’ve opted into.
  • To maintain compliance with GDPR and respect your consent preferences.

We will never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: When you subscribe to our newsletter or marketing list.
  • Legitimate Interest: When necessary to maintain the operation and security of our website.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clicking the Unsubscribe link in any email or contacting us at contact@gentlemens-order.com.

4. Data Storage and Retention

We store your information securely and only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined above. If you unsubscribe, your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal, essential cookies to ensure proper website functionality. If we ever use analytics or marketing cookies, we will request your explicit consent first in compliance with EU cookie regulations.

You can control cookie preferences via your browser settings at any time.

6. Data Sharing

We may share limited data with trusted third-party services that help us operate our website and email communications, including:

  • MailerLite – for managing our email newsletters.
  • Stripe – for processing payments (if applicable).

These providers comply with GDPR and process data only under our instructions.

7. Your Rights (EU Residents)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@gentlemens-order.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

9. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date. Please review it occasionally to stay informed.
